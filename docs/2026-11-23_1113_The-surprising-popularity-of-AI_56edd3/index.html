  • Surveys across 34 countries show that nearly nine in ten people in Nigeria and Vietnam believe AI will improve humanity, while rich Western countries are much gloomier.
  • In America, Canada and France more people feel negatively than positively about AI's future impact, and even net-positive Western countries are far less optimistic than poor ones.
